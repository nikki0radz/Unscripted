Connect AI

The recorder and archive work without AI. To enable automatic transcription, summaries and questions, connect your private AI backend.

Your OpenAI API key belongs on the backend. By default this access code stays only in this tab; opting in stores it in this browser, so only do so on a trusted device and dedicated diary domain.

Your recordings stay yours

Audio, notes and entries live in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data, changing devices or losing your phone can erase them. Export regular backups.

Backups include audio and are not encrypted. Store them somewhere private. Import merges entries with matching IDs.

About recording

The app saves short, independently playable audio clips while you record. Please keep the app open and your phone awake when recording. Check available device storage for long sessions. You can pause, resume and add separate recordings later.
