Private cloud backups

Your recordings and diary text are encrypted on this device before they go to your private cloud storage. Your diary link alone cannot unlock them.

Use a different password from your diary access code. Keep it in your password manager: it cannot be recovered. Unlock once each time you open the app; automatic backups then run while the app stays open. The password is never uploaded or saved in browser storage.

Keeps seven recent snapshots per device. Deleting a local entry does not remove its copies from cloud backups. Manage permanent cloud removal in Cloudflare. This is backup and restore, not live sync between devices.

Your recordings stay yours

Audio, notes and entries live in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data, changing devices or losing your phone can erase them. Export regular backups.

Backups include audio and are not encrypted. Store them somewhere private. Import merges entries with matching IDs.

Private sign-in

At your private Cloudflare address, your diary access code signs this browser in for seven days. Sign out on a shared device. Local recordings remain stored on this device.

Require fingerprint, face or device PIN when you open or return to this app. Applies to this device.

Your phone uses a passkey, unlocked by fingerprint, face or device PIN. Keep your diary access code as a fallback. Your backup recovery password stays separate.

Connect AI

The recorder and archive work without AI. To enable automatic transcription, summaries and questions, connect your private AI backend.

Your OpenAI API key belongs on the backend. By default this access code stays only in this tab; opting in stores it in this browser, so only do so on a trusted device and dedicated diary domain.

Install Unscripted

Keep Unscripted on your home screen and open it like an app, without browser tabs. AI can be connected separately whenever you are ready.

On Android, you can also open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Recordings are still stored on this device: installing does not back them up or sync them.

About recording

The app saves short, independently playable audio clips while you record. Please keep the app open and your phone awake when recording. Check available device storage for long sessions. You can pause, resume and add separate recordings later.
