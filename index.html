Privacy & sign-inApp locking & account access⌄

Private sign-in

At your private Cloudflare address, your diary access code signs this browser in for seven days. Sign out on a shared device. Local recordings remain stored on this device.

App & recordingVersion, installation & recording information⌄

App updates

Checks when you open the Android app. Downloads are optional, and Android asks before installing.

Install Unscripted

Keep Unscripted on your home screen and open it like an app, without browser tabs. AI can be connected separately whenever you are ready.

On Android, you can also open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Recordings are still stored on this device: installing does not back them up or sync them.

About recording

The app saves short, independently playable audio clips while you record. Please keep the app open and your phone awake when recording. Check available device storage for long sessions. You can pause, resume and add separate recordings later.
